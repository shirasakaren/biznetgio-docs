The [Terraform](https://github.com/shirasakaren/terraform-provider-biznetgio) and [Pulumi](https://github.com/shirasakaren/pulumi-biznetgio) providers documented on this site cover five product lines: [NEO Lite](/products/neolite), [NEO Lite Pro](/products/neolite-pro), [NEO Metal](/products/baremetal) (including its Elastic Storage and additional-IP add-ons), [NEO GPU](/products/gpu), and [NEO Object Storage](/products/object-storage). Everything below is a real Biznet GIO service with real, current pricing, useful for cost comparison - but none of it is reachable through either provider's source code. Order it through the [Biznet GIO portal](https://portal.biznetgio.com) or by contacting Biznet GIO directly.
