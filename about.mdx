## Who maintains this

Shirasaka Ren is an infrastructure engineer working across DevOps, Security, Cloud, and Servers. Her motto: *"Infrastructure that stays boring, on purpose"* - her favorite compliment is silence: no incidents, no surprises, no 3AM pages.

She started her career in October 2022 and works on clusters, pipelines, and the boring-on-purpose infrastructure underneath them - across startups, research labs, and data centres. In her words: *"I keep the lights on."*

The Biznet GIO providers come from her own IaC practice: automating NEO Lite VPS fleets, NEO Metal bare-metal, NEO GPU rentals, and NEO Object Storage with Terraform and Pulumi.
